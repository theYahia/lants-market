# Privacy & Terms

## Privacy

This interface is static and non-custodial. It never touches your keys or funds —
every action is a transaction you sign in your own wallet.

- The wallet modal is Privy (privy.io), a React SDK bundled into this page; it talks to Privy servers (auth.privy.io) when the modal opens and a wallet connects. Login is wallet-only — no email, no social accounts, no embedded wallets — and connecting a phone by QR uses the public WalletConnect relay through the WalletConnect project ID configured by Privy.
- We use no analytics, no logging, no cookies. We do not collect or store personal data ourselves.
- Anything you send to the blockchain — addresses, listings, trades — is
  public and permanent. We can't delete or alter it, and neither can anyone else.
