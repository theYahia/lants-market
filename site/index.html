List a position

Staking rewards for the open epoch can't be claimed before listing and pass to the buyer with the NFT. Claim now for non-transferable ANTS, or restake to mint a sellable position.
Marketplace fee is 1%: the seller receives the price minus 1%.

Manage a position

Split, move to another seller pool, or enable max-lock. New positions start at the next epoch and can't exit until then (PositionChangePending).
Final: a max-locked position can no longer be split or moved. Disabling starts a fresh 104-week countdown from the next epoch and makes the position movable again.
Stake buyer reward
Restake pending buyer rewards for an epoch into a seller pool (mints a new position). Callable by the buyer or its operator.
